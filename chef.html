The Chef

Cooking with fire & patience.

A kitchen built around one wood-fired hearth, and the people who tend it every night.

Head Chef & Co-founder

Marcus Vale

Marcus trained in classical kitchens before spending several years cooking over open fire in small coastal restaurants. He opened Aurelia in 2014 with the idea that a restaurant should feel like the best dinner party you have ever been to.

He still writes the menu every week, usually after a morning at the market, and he works the pass himself most nights.

In the Kitchen

How the kitchen works.

  1. 01

    Start with the grower

    Every dish begins with a conversation with the people who grow, catch or make it.

  2. 02

    Cook with the fire

    Embers, smoke and flame do the seasoning; sauces stay light and clear.

  3. 03

    Finish at the pass

    Each plate is checked and finished by a chef before it reaches the dining room.
