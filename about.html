Our Story

Eight tables and one hearth.

Aurelia began in 2014 as eight tables around a single wood-fired hearth on Linden Row. We wanted a dining room where nobody watches the clock: one seating per table, every night.

The menu is written each week with a small circle of growers, fishers and cheesemakers within a few hours of the kitchen. When something is at its best, it goes on the menu. When it is gone, so is the dish.

Everything that can be cooked over fire is: vegetables blistered on the grill, fish finished over embers, bread baked in the dying heat after service.

Philosophy

Three things we never compromise on.

  1. 01

    Seasonal, always

    We write the menu with our growers each week and cook only what is at its peak.

  2. 02

    Fire at the centre

    One oak-fired hearth shapes the kitchen: slow embers for fish, high flame for meat and vegetables.

  3. 03

    Unhurried service

    One seating per table. Your table is yours for the evening, from the first glass to the last coffee.

The Room

Candlelight, river views and forty-eight seats.

The dining room looks out over the river through tall warehouse windows. Tables are set well apart so conversation stays at the table.

Our bar seats eight and pours a short list of classic cocktails, low-intervention wines and non-alcoholic pairings.
